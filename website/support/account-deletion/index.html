Request account
and data deletion.

Contact Ragent support to request deletion of your Ragent account and associated data, or to ask about particular records.

Requests are reviewed manually. This page does not immediately erase an account. The current Android candidate has no verified automatic account-erasure workflow.

How to make a request

  1. Email iamandyusuk@gmail.com, from the email associated with your Ragent account if it has one; otherwise provide your Ragent username.
  2. Use the subject Ragent account deletion request. State whether you want to delete the account and associated data or request deletion of specific data.
  3. Support may ask you to verify ownership. Do not send any account passwords, API keys, authentication tokens or government identity documents.
  4. Wait for confirmation of the requested scope and outcome. Sending the email, signing out, unlinking a device or uninstalling the app does not by itself confirm account deletion.
Start an email request

Scope of the request

Specify whether the request covers your account identity, device and computer associations, authorization records, synchronized configuration or other account-associated records. Support must identify any records that require separate processing or retention and explain the outcome.

Data on a computer you manage, exported files, backups and content already sent to another model provider or app are separate copies. They are not automatically erased by deleting the phone app, and an offline computer cannot be assumed to have received a deletion request.

Processing status

There is no self-service deletion status endpoint on this support site. Use the email conversation to request an update. A support acknowledgment is different from confirmation that account data has been erased.
