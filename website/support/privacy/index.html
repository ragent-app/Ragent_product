Privacy notice

Updated 13 November 2026

Ragent includes an Android client and computer workbenches for Windows, macOS and Linux. The data involved depends on the features you use, the computer you connect to, and the model providers you configure.

Who to contact

This notice applies to Ragent. For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests, contact iamandyusuk@gmail.com.

Other services and recipients

Google sign-in, when chosen, and Firebase messaging are provided by Google. Desktop update checks and downloads use GitHub Releases, which receives the network information needed to provide those services. Your selected model providers, workbench host and task integrations may process data under their own privacy terms. Files you explicitly share with another app are then handled by that app. The current Android candidate does not include an advertising SDK or request the Android advertising ID permission.

Optional cloud speech recognition

Cloud speech recognition is optional. If you choose to use it, the Android app sends your recording to Ragent Accounts. Accounts checks your Ragent account, consent and monthly usage limit, then forwards the recording to OpenAI's Audio Transcriptions API (gpt-transcribe) to transcribe it in its spoken language. The transcript is added to your draft only; it is not sent as a message automatically.

Ragent does not store the recording or transcript in its database. To support a safe retry, a transcript may remain in Ragent Accounts server memory for up to five minutes. Ragent stores account-linked usage and request information such as device ID, recording duration and size, an audio hash, request status, detected language, timing and estimated provider cost. Normal completed or failed request records are deleted after 90 days. Requests whose provider outcome cannot be confirmed are not automatically removed after 90 days and can remain until the account is deleted. Monthly usage totals remain until account deletion. A minimal record of consent (account and device identifiers, disclosure version, action and time) is kept for up to five years, including after account deletion.

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train or improve its models unless the API customer explicitly opts in. OpenAI's API data controls and the applicable API account/project settings and terms govern provider processing. See OpenAI's API data controls and the OpenAI Services Agreement.

You can decline or revoke consent at any time. Cloud speech recognition is unavailable without your consent; other Ragent features remain available.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local drafts, task records and results may remain on your phone or computer until removed through the relevant application or device controls. Removing the Android app does not by itself delete account records or data on a separate computer.

Account and binding records may remain in the account service until a deletion request is verified and processed. Authorization tokens and temporary grants have their own validity periods; their expiry is different from erasing account records. Limited operational or security records may need separate handling. There is no claim that an offline computer is erased immediately or that all copies share one retention period.

Use the account and data request instructions to contact support about deletion. Requests are reviewed manually; a request acknowledgment is not a completed deletion receipt. The applicable scope, verification and any remaining records must be explained when a request is processed.

This support website

This website is hosted on our server and delivered through Cloudflare. The hosting and delivery services process ordinary web request information, such as IP addresses, request paths and browser headers, to serve pages and protect the site. The site itself has no account sign-in or file-upload form. Contact links open your own email application.

Your choices

You can choose which computer and model provider receive a task, which files or text to submit, whether to grant optional camera and notification permissions, and whether to share an output with another application. Ending a connection or signing out is different from deleting an account and its associated data.

Young users

Ragent is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact support so the request can be investigated.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the app, its services or data practices change. The update date appears at the top of this page.
